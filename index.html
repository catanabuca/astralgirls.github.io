THE PROJECT

Making Earth data something you can feel

Astral Girls · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

Astral Girls is an interactive sonification concept for the Earth Information Jukebox challenge. It translates patterns in Earth-system observations into sound, giving people another way to notice relationships in a complex planet: not just by looking at a chart, but by listening to change.

01 / EXPLORE

Read the planet

Start with environmental indicators such as temperature anomaly, ocean conditions and cloud cover. Each signal represents a different part of the Earth system and can be explored independently or together.

OBSERVATION → INPUT
02 / TRANSLATE

Turn patterns into sound

Each variable is assigned a musical behaviour. The mapping is designed to be understandable and repeatable, so a change in the data creates a meaningful change in the listening experience.

INPUT → SOUND RULES
03 / UNDERSTAND

Listen and compare

Adjust the controls, hear how the soundscape responds, and compare calmer and more dynamic conditions. The goal is curiosity and data literacy—not replacing scientific analysis.

SOUND → INSIGHT
OUR SONIFICATION DESIGN

Three signals. Three musical dimensions.

Temperature shapes tonal height, ocean activity shapes rhythmic motion, and cloud cover shapes the atmospheric layer. Optional birdsong and pollution-inspired audio layers let listeners contrast a natural soundscape with an urban or industrial acoustic texture.

Data transparency: This version is an interactive prototype. The values and sliders are illustrative controls, not live NASA observations. A production data pipeline would connect documented NASA and other authoritative Earth-observation datasets, preserve units and timestamps, and show the source and period for every displayed value.
